## 研究问题与用途

研究怎样组织化学对象、条件、关系与观测，支持可检查的推理，并比较不同机理解释。材料燃烧与生烟提供案例，化学推理方法本身作为独立研究方向推进。

目标是给出有依据的候选解释，保留假设和未知部分，并说明哪些证据可能改变判断。

## 已有模型能力

- **可执行关系表达：**已有化学关系与局部过程的表达和运行原型，能够检查推理所依赖的信息。
- **证据组织与追溯：**已有文献依据整理、引用回查和解释支持检查的研究实现。
- **候选解释比较：**已有局部学习模型与候选解释原型，探索不同解释的比较和评价。
- **研究展示：**已有推理关系、证据、假设和竞争解释的展示原型，便于逐项检查。

## 当前进度

局部推理模型、文献证据组织和解释展示已运行，部分真实文献案例完成开发验证。已经开展局部模型训练与比较，完整的证据判别、解释排序及跨问题能力仍需继续评价。

已有案例说明程序能够产生和检查候选推理，并不代表每条候选路径已被实验确认。不同来源的证据、作者解释与模型推断分别记录。

## 正在推进的工作

1. 补充结果变化、条件变化及竞争解释的独立案例。
2. 完善证据判别和候选解释评价，检查模型是否真正使用有区分度的信息。
3. 比较不同问题和材料条件下的表现，整理未知前提及失败边界。

形式执行、预测表现和化学机制成立分别评价。

## English

### Research question and intended use

This area studies how chemical objects, conditions, relations, and observations can support inspectable reasoning and comparison of mechanistic explanations. Material combustion and smoke provide cases, while the chemical reasoning method is developed as an independent research area.

The aim is to offer grounded candidate explanations, retain assumptions and unknowns, and indicate evidence that could change the judgment.

### Implemented capabilities

- **Executable relation representations:** Prototypes represent and execute chemical relations and local processes, enabling checks of the information on which reasoning depends.
- **Evidence organization and tracing:** Implementations organize literature evidence, check references, and examine support for explanations.
- **Comparison of candidate explanations:** Local learned models and explanation prototypes explore comparisons and evaluation of alternatives.
- **Research displays:** Prototypes display reasoning relations, evidence, assumptions, and competing explanations for inspection.

### Current progress

Local reasoning models, literature evidence organization, and explanation displays have run. Some real literature cases have undergone development evaluation. Local training and comparisons have been conducted; complete evidence assessment, explanation ranking, and generalization across questions require further evaluation.

The cases demonstrate that programs can generate and inspect candidate reasoning, rather than confirming every candidate path experimentally. Source evidence, authors' interpretations, and model inferences are recorded separately.

### Ongoing work

1. Add independent cases with changing outcomes, conditions, and competing explanations.
2. Improve evidence assessment and evaluation of candidate explanations, checking whether models use discriminative information.
3. Compare performance across questions and material conditions, and organize unknown premises and failure boundaries.

Formal execution, predictive performance, and confirmation of chemical mechanisms are evaluated separately.
