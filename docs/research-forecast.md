## 研究问题与用途

研究跨领域知识及其变化能否帮助发现具有后续发展潜力的科学问题，并支持研究方向判断。方法关注现有研究趋势之外的信息价值，烟科学作为首个案例领域。

希望形成可以评价的方向预测与推荐任务，并用具体案例解释推荐的科学意义及适用条件。

## 已有工作与能力

- **文献信息接入：**已建立真实文献题录的只读接入与整理入口，支持按研究主题组织材料。
- **研究问题组织：**已有方向卡与问题族的开发表示，用于比较相关研究问题。
- **预测与方法对照：**首版评价和轻量模型对照已经实现，支持真实文献材料上的开发回放。
- **证据与解释研究：**已整理跨领域证据组织和方向判断的相关原型，用于后续检验知识连接的贡献。

## 当前进度

首版量化评价、模型对照和真实开发回放已完成。当前结果尚未确认相对常规趋势方法的稳定增益，部分材料仍不足以单独检验跨领域信息的贡献。

正在完善研究问题的颗粒度、文献与问题的对应关系，以及评价数据的覆盖。回顾性开发与真实前瞻预测分开记录，当前开发结果不作为已经兑现的未来预测。

## 正在推进的工作

1. 扩充独立研究问题，补充可比较的单领域与跨领域证据。
2. 核对文献是否回应相应问题，改善方向与评价结果的对应关系。
3. 加入更强的方法对照，在新材料或真实前瞻任务中评价预测贡献。

方向发展潜力、论文活动与具体科学结论各有自己的证据范围，推荐结果不直接证明科学机制。

## English

### Research question and intended use

This area studies whether cross-domain knowledge and its changes help discover scientific questions with future development potential and support research direction decisions. The method examines information beyond existing research trends, with smoke science as the first case domain.

The aim is to build evaluable forecasting and recommendation tasks, and use concrete cases to explain their scientific significance and applicability.

### Existing work and capabilities

- **Literature access:** Read-only access and organization of real literature metadata support thematic research materials.
- **Research problem organization:** Development representations of direction cards and problem families support comparisons of related questions.
- **Forecasting and method comparisons:** An initial evaluation and lightweight model comparisons have been implemented for retrospective development evaluation using real literature materials.
- **Evidence and explanation research:** Related prototypes organize cross-domain evidence and research judgments for subsequent evaluation of knowledge connections.

### Current progress

The initial quantitative evaluation, model comparisons, and retrospective development evaluation have been completed. Current results have not established a stable advantage over conventional trend methods. Some materials are insufficient to test the contribution of cross-domain information independently.

Research problem granularity, the correspondence between literature and questions, and evaluation coverage are being improved. Retrospective development and prospective forecasting are recorded separately; development results are not presented as realized future predictions.

### Ongoing work

1. Expand independent research problems and comparable single-domain and cross-domain evidence.
2. Check whether literature addresses the relevant questions and improve the mapping between directions and outcomes.
3. Add stronger method comparisons and evaluate forecasting contributions on new materials or genuinely prospective tasks.

Direction development potential, publication activity, and specific scientific conclusions have distinct evidence requirements. Recommendations do not directly establish scientific mechanisms.
